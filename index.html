Rules

Based on Battle Angel Alita: Last Order - Chapter 121

  • The board is a 5x5 grid full of Rescue and Death buttons
  • You start with 8 lives, including yourself
  • You start with 16 buttons, half Rescue and half Death
  • Press a button within 30 seconds or a random life is lost
  • Each button press removes a button from the board of the same type
  • After round 4, a Merciless button appears (💀)
  • The Merciless button kills a random non-player
  • If you die, everyone dies
  • Survive 8 rounds to win
